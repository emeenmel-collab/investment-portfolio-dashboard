Methodology & limitations
  • Market quotes and historical candles are retrieved through your server-side Worker and data-provider account.
  • Price availability, latency, history access, and usage limits depend on your provider plan.
  • Historical reconstruction uses current share counts at past closing prices. It is not actual account performance and excludes trades, cash flows, fees, taxes, and dividends.
  • Concentration index is the sum of squared percentage weights; higher values indicate a more concentrated portfolio.
  • Not investment advice. Verify market-data licensing and provider terms before publishing.
